# AE keyframing: user wishlist, popular plugins, and Adobe's native changes (2023–Oct 2026)

Scope note for the report writer: this is the user-perspective / prioritisation input for a team building an AE-class motion app. All dates below are as stated in the sources. Current as of 2026-10-09. Important access caveat: the research environment's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches for helpx.adobe.com, community.adobe.com, aescripts.com, schoolofmotion.com, cgchannel.com, provideocoalition.com, reddit.com and most other relevant domains, so every finding below is taken from search-engine excerpts of those pages (quotes are verbatim from the excerpts) rather than from full-page reads. Vote counts on Adobe Ideas were mostly not visible in excerpts; where a number is given it is noted explicitly. The per-session web-search budget was also exhausted before a few follow-ups (see Gaps).

## Key Question 1: Feature requests and complaints about keyframing (Adobe Ideas, Creative COW, Reddit, social, SoM/MDS)

### Takeaway
The loudest, most recent and most specific complaints cluster around four things: (1) the graph editor being "outdated, clunky, and untouched for over a decade" with no live preview and no built-in bounce/elastic/overshoot eases, (2) wanting ease copy/paste independent of values so badly that users asked Adobe to bundle EaseCopy, (3) staggering/offsetting keyframes (now partly native via Quick Offset, but single-layer stagger is still an acknowledged open request), and (4) keyframe hygiene issues (select-all in graph editor, snapping keys to whole frames, Option-drag duplicate, labels/grouping, timeline slowdowns with many keys). Reddit and X/Bluesky threads did not surface through the available search tool; the Adobe Community "Ideas"/feature-request board and Creative COW supplied the quotable primary posts.

### Cited Findings

Graph editor and easing
- A June 2025 Adobe Ideas post titled "After Effects: A Legacy Tool in a Modern World" states: "Graph Editor – still outdated, clunky, and untouched for over a decade. No real-time preview, no modular behavior, no intelligent UI." and "No modern easing tools – no built-in bounce, elastic, inertia, or overshoot. You still need to rely on Expression tricks or 3rd-party scripts for basic animation finesse." The requested fixes include user-defined easing presets, preview curves, and velocity visualization. — [Adobe Community Ideas (idi-p/15373747)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/after-effects-a-legacy-tool-in-a-modern-world/idi-p/15373747); duplicate listing at [feature-requests-530 (1215080)](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-530/after-effects-a-legacy-tool-in-a-modern-world-1215080). Search excerpts described the two copies as "roughly 16 to 13 months old" as of Oct 2026 (i.e. mid-2025); no vote count was visible.
- An Adobe Ideas post (listed as ~311 days old on 2026-10-09, i.e. c. December 2025) asks for a keyboard shortcut to "select all visible keys in the graph editor"; the poster notes a command exists that selects all visible keyframes on selected layers or all keyframes on all layers, but argues a graph-editor-scoped shortcut is still missing. An Adobe employee replied "That feature could be a great addition to After Effects." — [Adobe Community Ideas (idi-p/15613831)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/select-all-keyframes-in-the-graph-editor/idi-p/15613831)
- A 2010 Creative COW thread ("Advanced easing on keyframes?") estimated the poster spent "half their After Effects time repeating the same easing task in the motion editor, manually building what would basically be a Quad, Quint, or Expo curve"; their ideal was "a keyframe right-click, or better a keyboard shortcut, offering a range of easy-ease options" (Sine, Expo, Circ, etc.). The only native workaround offered was Cmd/Ctrl-Shift-K (Keyframe Velocity dialog). — [Creative COW](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/advanced-easing-on-keyframesae/)
- Older Creative COW threads (2008–2019) show the same recurring complaint: one poster wanted "a curve stronger than the 'easy ease' setting" and asked whether Easy Ease could be customized; another wanted a default influence value other than the stock setting and had searched the preferences file without finding one. — [Creative COW (?p=1876184)](https://creativecow.net/?p=1876184); [Creative COW (?p=156140)](https://creativecow.net/?p=156140)
- A 2006 Creative COW thread notes "temporal and spatial interpolation are still separate (unlike in 3D apps, where they are normally locked together)" and asks for spline-like smooth curves through keyframes. — [Creative COW: Graph editor spline interpolation](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/graph-editor-spline-interpolation/)
- In the aescripts Flow support thread, users "asked for a live preview of the easing before applying it"; the developers "said they were considering this for a future version of Flow". Other users reported "Flow became absolutely unusable due to extreme sluggishness" and confusion caused by the "Apply As Expressions" vs "Apply As Keyframes" setting. — [aescripts Flow forum p4](https://aescripts.com/forums/discussion/1340/flow/p4); [p5](https://aescripts.com/forums/discussion/1340/flow/p5)
- An Adobe Community "ease" discussion (thread 15081898, undated in excerpt) has an expert recommending Flow because it "gives you a more intuitive graph editor" than Ease and Wizz, plus EaseCopy to "copy/paste easing from one property to another" — i.e. community experts themselves route users to third-party tools for easing. — [Adobe Community discussion](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/ease/td-p/15081898)
- Third-party product copy explicitly frames the native gap: Kease "goes beyond standard two-handle curves, which makes anticipation, overshoot, bounce, and elastic motions easy to achieve and reusable as presets" and has "mirrored handles, so moving one handle automatically mirrors the opposite one". — [aescripts: Kease](https://aescripts.com/kease/); Easify 2 Pro advertises "a graph editor, unlimited presets, mirrored curves, and copy and paste easing, with customizable Back, Elastic, and Bounce curves". — [aescripts: Easify 2 Pro](https://aescripts.com/easify-pro)

Copying eases / bundling scripts
- February 2023 Adobe Ideas post "Ship EaseCopy plugin standard with AE": "EaseCopy is about the plugin I use the most on AE. It's free too!"; the poster said they "couldn't imagine a user who wouldn't keep it docked in their workspace", cited IT-bureaucracy friction installing scripts on work machines, and listed other free and paid plugins (incl. Overlord) they thought should come standard. Status "Open for Voting"; one copy showed ~2,575 views, the other ~2.6K views. A community-legend reply: "Not going to happen. There's a ton of legal red tape getting in the way." — [Adobe Community Ideas (idi-p/13563168)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/ship-easecopy-plugin-standard-with-ae/idi-p/13563168); [feature-requests-530 (1214155)](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-530/ship-easecopy-plugin-standard-with-ae-1214155)
- Adobe Ideas "More accessible scripts" asks for a built-in script launcher; a commenter pointed to third-party launchers as the existing option. — [Adobe Community feature request (1215159)](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-530/more-accessible-scripts-1215159)

Staggering / offsetting
- Adobe's Quick Offset "Feature Focus" thread (beta announced June 2025; "now released in the official release of After Effects 25.4") documents limitations users immediately hit: marquee-selected keyframes offset in layer order regardless of selection direction; one user wrote "you can only snap to the first or last layers and first or last keyframes when holding shift. It would be nice to be able to snap the middle layers and keyframes to the playhead too"; another asked for a dialog to type stagger values after invoking the shortcut. — [Adobe Community beta discussion (td-p/15377178)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-beta-discussions/feature-focus-quick-offset-stagger-your-layers-just-by-dragging-them/td-p/15377178); [page 2](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-beta-discussions/feature-focus-quick-offset-stagger-your-layers-just-by-dragging-them/td-p/15377178/page/2)
- A late-August 2025 bug report ("stagger isn't behave like expected") drew a September 2025 Adobe reply: Quick Offset "only works across multiple layers and it considered each layer a unit. For example, when selecting keyframes across multiple layers, all keyframes on the same layer move together." and "adding staggering with multiple keyframes in a single layer is a great feature request and we'll be looking into it!" Thread status: acknowledged. — [Adobe Community bug (idc-p/15484699)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-bugs/stagger-isn-t-behave-like-expected/idc-p/15484699); [bug-reports-528 (1216296)](https://community.adobe.com/bug-reports-528/stagger-isn-t-behave-like-expected-1216296)
- An older Adobe Community thread "Jitter keyframe timing?" asked for AE to "randomly stagger keyframe positions in time by a user-defined amount, through expressions or some command" (the poster noted wiggle exists for values but there is no time-based equivalent and wanted a non-destructive method); the reply recommended the aescripts tool Rift. — [Adobe Community (td-p/10532798)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects/jitter-keyframe-timing/td-p/10532798)
- Frame.io's December 13, 2023 "Insider Tips" article is devoted entirely to "how to create a staggered layer sequence in After Effects", evidence that staggering was still a tutorial-worthy workaround pre-25.4. — [Frame.io blog](https://blog.frame.io/2023/12/13/insider-tips-how-to-create-a-staggered-layer-sequence-in-after-effects/)

Copy/paste across layers and navigation (requests that Adobe has since closed)
- Adobe Ideas "Copy paste keyframes of multiple layers at the same time": older AE could not copy keyframes from several layers at once (scripts were the workaround); Adobe engineering replied "The ability to copy/cut/paste keyframes across multiple layers has now been released with version 24.4 of After Effects. You can also now Paste Reversed Keyframes, including across multiple layers." — [Adobe Community Ideas (idc-p/14617155)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/copy-paste-keyframes-of-multiple-layers-at-the-same-time/idc-p/14617155); [feature-requests-530 (1215294)](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-530/copy-paste-keyframes-of-multiple-layers-at-the-same-time-1215294). The same request appears in a 2008 Adobe wishlist thread ("keyframes on multiple selected layers to be copied and pasted onto their respective layers, where currently the layers themselves get copied"). — [Adobe wishlist thread (index12)](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/after-effects-wishlist-67159/index12.html)
- A 2022 Adobe Ideas post asked for a modifier so J/K keyframe navigation only considers selected layers, because "there were often too many keyframes on a timeline to make plain J and K useful"; Adobe shipped Shift+J/Shift+K in 23.0. — [Adobe Community Ideas (m-p/13229473)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/add-option-for-j-k-hotkeys-to-work-with-only-selected-layer-s/m-p/13229473); related request [feature-requests-530 (1214081)](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-530/improvements-to-the-keyboard-shortcuts-for-go-to-previous-next-keyframes-1214081)

Keyframe hygiene, snapping, duplication, labelling
- 2022 Adobe Ideas "Option-drag to duplicate keyframes and layers": "in a lot of Adobe programs, you can hold 'option' (on mac) and drag a shape or layer to duplicate it"; commenters want the same for keyframes "as in Premiere". — [Adobe Community Ideas (idi-p/13229572, page 2)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/option-drag-to-duplicate-keyframes-and-layers/idi-p/13229572/page/2)
- "Snapping all keyframes to a full frame": a user had to move "around 50 keyframes manually" after a frame-rate change put keys on half frames and said "a native After Effects command would be useful"; a third-party script does it but "does not preserve keyframe easing". — [Adobe Community (54655)](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/snapping-all-keysframes-to-a-full-frame-54655); [discussion (m-p/14378418)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/snapping-all-keysframes-to-a-full-frame/m-p/14378418/highlight/true)
- "AE playhead not snapping on the next keyframe": "Pressing J and K moves to keyframes, but in some cases the playhead does not fall on the exact keyframe and goes in between." — [Adobe Community (td-p/11184419)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/ae-playhead-not-snapping-on-the-next-keyframe/td-p/11184419); Creative COW users note the UI "always snaps keyframes to frame boundaries" yet Alt-stretching "does not snap those keyframes to the boundary" (inconsistent behaviour). — [Creative COW (?p=235503)](https://creativecow.net/?p=235503); "Allow keyframes between frames" thread — [Adobe Community (m-p/13450270)](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/allow-keyframes-between-frames/m-p/13450270)
- Long-running Adobe wishlist threads (2008–2012) ask for: keyframe labels/grouping "like Nuke's backdrop node, so that groups of keyframes within a layer could be labelled" (2012); "color labels for keyframes could be handy sometimes" (2008); "a keyboard shortcut to view only keyframes, not keyframes and expressions as it is now" (2009). — [Adobe wishlist (index6)](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/after-effects-wishlist-67159/index6.html); [index8](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/after-effects-wishlist-67159/index8.html); [pre-Sept-09 wishlist index22](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/after-effects-wish-list-prior-to-sept-09-61806/index22.html)
- Sander van Dijk's 2015 "Imagining a better After Effects" collected "dozens of ideas for improvements" with a public voting system; his keyframes page lists items such as "Graph Editor Show Selected Properties by default", "Lock Keyframes to In and Out Points", and a thick line connecting keyframes that change value "to show where movement happens" (attribution of those three items to this page is inferred from the search excerpt). — [Motionographer, 17 Sep 2015](https://motionographer.com/2015/09/17/imagining-a-better-after-effects-with-sander-van-dijk/); [sandervandijk.tv keyframes](http://www.sandervandijk.tv/after-effects-features/keyframes)

Performance / bulk editing
- Bug report dated August 11, 2026: "Timeline UI gets progressively slower as more keyframes are selected with marquee selection"; the reporter saw slowdown start at about 100 selected keyframes and said it tracks selection size, not total keyframe count. No fix status visible. — [Adobe Community bug (1636432)](https://community.adobe.com/bug-reports-528/timeline-ui-gets-progressively-slower-as-more-keyframes-are-selected-with-marquee-selection-1636432)
- Proportional Scrubbing (beta 26.2x25) applies only to timeline values; asked whether it works in the Properties panel, Adobe answered "At the moment, no. But please let us know if you have interest in that." — [Adobe Community beta announcement (1550451)](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-532/new-in-beta-proportional-scrubbing-for-values-in-the-timeline-1550451)
- The Properties panel's multi-keyframe editing behaviour was itself a complaint until fixed: Adobe's fixed-issues list for 26.3 (June 2026) says "Changing values via the Properties only affects the current time, no longer sets all selected keyframes to the same value", repeated for 26.5. — [Adobe fixed issues](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/troubleshooting/fixed-and-known-issues/fixed-issues.html)

- Adobe's April 8, 2025 Bluesky debut drew "over 1,600 angry comments from digital creators" and Adobe deleted its posts; the criticism was about pricing and AI policies, not keyframing. ### Inferences
- The pattern across 2006–2026 is remarkably stable: easing presets/overshoot, ease copy/paste, stagger, and graph-editor ergonomics recur in every era, and Adobe has mostly closed the *navigation/copy-paste* class (Shift+J/K, multi-layer copy, paste reversed, Quick Offset) while leaving the *easing/graph editor* class to third parties.
- The 2023 "ship EaseCopy with AE" request and the "legal red tape" reply are strong evidence that ease copy/paste is perceived as table-stakes, and that a new app shipping it natively would be differentiating.
- Adobe's own replies ("great feature request", "please let us know if you have interest") on single-layer stagger and Properties-panel proportional scrubbing indicate these are live, unclosed asks as of 2025–2026.
- Reddit/X/Bluesky threads almost certainly exist but could not be retrieved; the Adobe Ideas board and Creative COW are the quotable primary record available here.

## Key Question 2: Popularity signals for keyframing plugins/scripts (aescripts, roundups, studio standards, free vs paid)

### Takeaway
No official aescripts bestseller ranking or review counts could be retrieved (site blocked; excerpts did not include ratings). The strongest available popularity signals are: aescripts' "Keyframes" tag holding 132 products; Flow ranked #2 of 20 in Spotlight FX's March 2026 roundup and reviewed by School of Motion; EaseCopy (free) appearing in essentially every 2025–2026 free-script roundup; Animation Composer claiming a very large free user base (~900k, unverified); Mt. Mograph Motion Studio ("50+ tools", with Stagger added March 2023 and a new Easing screen December 2023) and Motion Design School's Motion Tools Pro ("30+ scripts", updated March 2026) as the two big paid toolkits; and KBar as the de facto launcher around which these tools are organised.

### Inferences
- Items 1–4 and 6–7 are the ones where AE users still pay (or install free scripts) in 2026 and where Adobe has shipped nothing; these are the highest-value defaults for a new app. Items 8–9 are now native in AE and should be treated as expected parity.
- Because Adobe's Quick Offset is the newest and most-discussed native change, matching and exceeding it (single-layer, curved, random, numeric, snap-to-playhead for middle items) is a visible, specific way to look "ahead of AE" to this audience.
- Evidence counts are a floor: Reddit/X/Bluesky could not be sampled, so items with heavy social discussion (easing, graph editor) are likely under-counted relative to forum-only items.

### Gaps
- Evidence counts are based on sources retrievable via search excerpts only; no vote totals from Adobe Ideas and no aescripts review counts were available to weight the ranking quantitatively.
- Dope-sheet summaries, popover easing UI and "magic easing" as explicit AE-user requests were not found; they appear only in descriptions of Cavalry.
- Whether the 2022 Option-drag-duplicate-keyframes idea has shipped could not be verified from release notes.
